Kalpitiya Salt Roads

Salt pans, lagoon edges, sandy village lanes, hidden beaches, and slow seafood stops around Kalpitiya.

01 day Coastal wild Easy / scenic

Route overview

A coastal backroad day built around salt pans, lagoon light, fishing villages, and sandy tracks.

This 3-day Kalpitiya coastal route is a private journey through Sri Lanka’s north-western fishing coast, combining beach landscapes, coastal village life, lagoon-side roads, camping, local fishing culture and dolphin watching. Starting from Colombo, the route travels through Puttalam towards Kalpitiya and Kudawa Beach before continuing along the Uchchimunai road to the camping point.

The first day introduces guests to the coastal character of Kalpitiya, with time around Kudawa Beach, traditional fishing activity and the lifestyle of fishermen families living along this unique stretch of coastline. The route also gives travellers a better understanding of the area’s geography, where the sea, lagoon, sand roads, fishing settlements and coastal wind shape everyday life.

The second day is slower and more relaxed, allowing guests to spend time around Kudawa Beach and the Kalpitiya coastal area. This day is designed for swimming, resting, walking along the beach and enjoying the quiet atmosphere of Sri Lanka’s north-west coast.

On the final day, the journey continues with one of Kalpitiya’s most memorable experiences: dolphin watching. Guests will also visit Kalpitiya Fort and the local fish market, adding historical and cultural context before returning through Puttalam to Colombo.

This route is ideal for travellers who want a coastal experience beyond the usual beach holiday. It brings together fishing culture, beach relaxation, camping, marine life, local markets and the raw coastal beauty of Kalpitiya in a carefully paced 3-day journey.

Route Map

Day 1: Colombo to Dutch Bay Resort via Puttalam, Kalpitiya and Uchchimunai North-west coast | Lagoon roads, fishing villages, Uchchimunai coastal scenery and Dutch Bay stay

Begin the journey from Colombo, travelling north through Puttalam towards the coastal region of Kalpitiya. As the route moves closer to the peninsula, the landscape begins to change into a mix of lagoon edges, sandy roads, fishing villages and open coastal scenery.

The day continues towards Uchchimunai, where guests experience the raw coastal geography of Kalpitiya’s north-western edge. This section introduces the character of the region through quiet roads, fishing settlements, lagoon-side views, sea breeze and the wide, open feeling of the Kalpitiya coastline.

After exploring the Uchchimunai side of the route, the journey returns to Dutch Bay Resort & Spa Kalpitiya for the first overnight stay. The day is designed as a scenic coastal introduction, combining the drive from Colombo, the changing dry-zone landscapes, local fishing surroundings and a comfortable resort stay by the coast.

Overnight stay Dutch Bay Resort & Spa Kalpitiya
  • Scenic drive from Colombo through Puttalam to Kalpitiya
  • Uchchimunai coastal route, lagoon edges and fishing-village scenery
  • Overnight stay at Dutch Bay Resort & Spa Kalpitiya
Day 2: Dolphin Watching, Lagoon Kayaking and Eluwankulama Backwaters Marine life and backwaters | Dolphins, hotel lagoon kayaking, Eluwankulama and The Backwaters Lodge

Day two begins with one of Kalpitiya’s most memorable coastal experiences: dolphin watching. Depending on sea conditions and seasonal movement, guests may have the opportunity to see dolphins in the waters off the Kalpitiya coast, making this one of the key highlights of the route.

After returning from the sea, the day continues with kayaking in the hotel lagoon, giving guests a calmer water-based experience surrounded by the coastal atmosphere of Kalpitiya. This part of the day balances the excitement of dolphin watching with a slower and more relaxed lagoon experience.

Later, the journey leaves the main Kalpitiya coast and continues towards Eluwankulama, where the landscape shifts into quieter backwater scenery, village roads and nature-based surroundings. The day ends with an overnight stay at The Backwaters Lodge, offering a peaceful change of atmosphere from the open coast.

Overnight stay The Backwaters Lodge Eluwamkulama, Sri Lanka
  • Dolphin watching off the Kalpitiya coast
  • Kayaking in the hotel lagoon
  • Eluwankulama backwater route and overnight stay at The Backwaters Lodge
Day 3: Gagewadiya Backwaters and Return to Colombo Final coastal backroads | Gagewadiya scenery, village landscapes and the return journey

The final day begins from The Backwaters Lodge and continues through the quieter waterside and village landscapes around Gagewadiya. This section gives the route a softer closing experience, with backwater scenery, rural surroundings and a slower look at the coastal life beyond Kalpitiya’s main beach areas.

As the journey moves through Gagewadiya, guests can experience the calm atmosphere of the area, shaped by water, village roads, fishing activity and the dry-zone coastal landscape. It is a simple but meaningful final section that keeps the route connected to the local geography and everyday life of the region.

The journey then continues back towards Colombo, completing a 3-day coastal route that combines Kalpitiya’s beaches, Uchchimunai, dolphin watching, lagoon kayaking, backwater landscapes, village scenery and two distinctive overnight stays.

  • Gagewadiya waterside and village landscapes
  • Backwater scenery and rural coastal surroundings
  • Return journey to Colombo
